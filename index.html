02Why Human-AI Interaction

AI works
through people.

AI systems don't act in isolation. People interpret their outputs, decide when to trust them, and try to guide them. A more capable model can still lead to worse outcomes if that interaction goes wrong.

The value—and the risk—often lives in the interaction layer, not just in the model.

03About the library

Research for
human-centered AI.

The HAII Library is a growing collection of research and resources on Human-AI Interaction, behavioral psychology, and decision-making.

Its purpose is to make useful knowledge easier to find, connect, and bring into conversations about how AI systems are designed and used.

Created by Irene H. Jack, with a background in Psychology and Cognitive Science and a focus on Human-AI Interaction.
