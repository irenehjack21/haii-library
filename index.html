02About

Understanding how
AI shapes us.

AI is changing how we think, decide and relate to one another. Making sense of that takes more than technology: it needs psychology, behavioral science and design.

This library gathers the resources that help connect those fields, in one place.

The HAII Library collects research and reading on Human-AI Interaction, with notes on what matters and why.
Why HAII is relevant for tech companies:

AI systems don't do anything in isolation, they do things through people. A model can be more capable on benchmarks yet produce worse outcomes because people misunderstand what it's doing, over-trust it, under-trust it, or can't steer it.

A lot of the value and a lot of the harm lives in the interaction layer, not the weights. Questions like how people calibrate trust, when they defer to a system versus override it, how explanations change behavior (often not in the direction you'd hope), and how automation reshapes skill over time are not soft add-ons. They determine whether a capable system actually helps.
